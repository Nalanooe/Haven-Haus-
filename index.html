About Haven Haus

Haven Haus was founded in 2025 as a luxury property management and turnover cleaning service based in Cape Town. We specialize in creating seamless guest-ready experiences for short-term rental hosts, bringing hotel-grade standards and hospitality excellence to every property we manage.

Our mission is to take the stress out of property management by providing consistent, reliable, and professional service that allows hosts to focus on growth while we ensure their properties remain immaculate and guest-ready.

With support for over 50 Airbnb properties across Cape Town, we've built our reputation on attention to detail, fast turnaround times, and seamless communication through a WhatsApp-first approach.
